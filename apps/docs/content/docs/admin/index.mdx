These guides are for the people who run a site once it is set up: adding pets, building pages, managing images, forms, people and settings. Everything here happens in the team admin at `/admin`. Setting up and hosting a site is in [Deploy](../developers/deployment/index.mdx).

## What you can do depends on your role

Each person who signs in has a role: **admin**, **editor**, **developer** or **audit**. The admin's sidebar only shows the screens your role can use, and a screen you may look at but not change opens read-only, with a notice at the top.

In short:

- **Pets, pages and media:** admins, editors and developers change them; audit can only look.
- **Theme and site settings:** admins and developers change them; audit can only look; editors don't see them.
- **Users and sign-in settings:** admins only.

[Users and roles](./users-and-roles.mdx) has the full table.

While the site can send email, every account must confirm its email address before it can change anything, so a new person may see a banner asking them to do that first.

## Where things are in the admin

The sidebar has three sections:

| Section | Screens |
| --- | --- |
| **Content** | **Pets**, **Pages** (with the page builder) and **Media** (the image library) |
| **Plugins** | **Forms**, **Instagram** and any other plugin the site uses |
| **Settings** | **Email**, **Theme**, **Users**, **Authentication**, **Activity** and **Site settings** |

The **Dashboard** shows recent activity and a card from each plugin.
